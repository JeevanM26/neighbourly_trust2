## Codebase Patterns
- Neighborly Trust is a split-frontend monorepo: Customer App (`src/`, port 3000), Worker App (`worker/`, port 3001), Admin App (`admin/`, port 3002).
- Shared Supabase PostgreSQL + PostGIS backend. Both customer and worker read/write the same tables.
- Category 3D icons are served either from Supabase Storage `category-icons` bucket or fallback `/categories/*.png`.
- Concierge Hotline phone: `7975182162` (configured via env or fallback).
- Customer profiles and bookings do not expose raw phone numbers across customer/worker roles; admin has full visibility for review and dispatch.
